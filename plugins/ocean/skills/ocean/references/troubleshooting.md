### Overview

> **Work in progress**
>
> The Ocean troubleshooting knowledge base is being prepared. This section
> currently acts as a reserved entry point and does not yet provide complete
> diagnostic coverage.

Troubleshooting knowledge will be organized around observable symptoms,
likely causes, diagnostic steps, and verified resolutions. Planned coverage
includes:

- Ocean DSL validation and parsing failures;
- application generation and build failures;
- configuration and dependency problems;
- broker, database, and integration connectivity;
- deployment and runtime failures;
- Ocean-Atlas publishing and search issues.

Until focused troubleshooting entries are published, consult the relevant
[DSL reference](dsl/overview.md) or [working examples](https://atlas.ocean-lab.ai/examples/). Problems that remain
unresolved can be reported through the
[Ocean-Atlas issue tracker](https://github.com/gilmardcom/ocean-atlas/issues).
